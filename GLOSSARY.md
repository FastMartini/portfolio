# Portfolio Experience

The portfolio presents Diego Martinez's work and identity to hiring managers and recruiters through a mountain-as-growth narrative.

## Language

**Mountain Journey**:
The portfolio's central narrative, framing Diego's personal and professional growth as an ascent through meaningful work and experiences.
_Avoid_: Timeline, career ladder

**Ascent**:
The Mountain Journey's scrolling experience, following a switchback trail up an illustrated mountain from Trailhead to Summit. Visitors scroll normally, with brief pauses at each stop and Waypoints to explore along the way.
_Avoid_: Reverse scroll, scroll-jacking

**Landmark**:
The illustration marking a Waypoint's place on the mountain, such as a watchtower, lodge, or high-pass sign.
_Avoid_: Project icon, decorative badge

**Trail Sign**:
The orientation card naming the current position along the Ascent and offering an action to open the current Waypoint.
_Avoid_: Toast, notification

**Waypoint Panel**:
The reading surface opened from a Waypoint's marker or Landmark, containing its summary, contribution, evidence, and available Case Study.
_Avoid_: Project popup, modal card

**List View**:
The complete full-text version of the portfolio, offering every Waypoint and published Case Study independently of the illustrated Ascent.
_Avoid_: Fallback page, plain mode

**Editorial Alpine**:
The portfolio's visual character, combining quiet editorial sophistication with restrained mountain details, generous space, and deliberate motion.
_Avoid_: Rugged outdoors, generic tech minimalism

**Trailhead**:
The opening of the Mountain Journey, introducing Diego, his professional focus, and clear actions to view selected work or contact him.
_Avoid_: Splash screen, intro animation

**Trail Rail**:
The vertical orientation aid running from Trailhead at the bottom to Summit at the top, with a navigable stop for each position along the Ascent. It replaces the former Route Indicator.
_Avoid_: Progress bar, game HUD

**Waypoint**:
A project or experience along the Mountain Journey that demonstrates growth, craft, or impact. Waypoints are ordered by relevance and strength of evidence rather than strict chronology.
_Avoid_: Project card, milestone

**Case Study**:
A focused account of a Waypoint that gives hiring managers deeper evidence of Diego's role, decisions, and outcomes.
_Avoid_: Project page, portfolio entry

**Compact Waypoint**:
A concise appearance in the Mountain Journey for a project that does not warrant a dedicated Case Study or lacks enough approved public evidence for one.
_Avoid_: Mini case study, filler project

**Personal Interlude**:
A brief, text-led section within the Mountain Journey that reveals Diego's interests, especially blues and electric guitar, without competing with the professional work. It may include one intimate photograph but no required audio experience.
_Avoid_: Hobbies page, lifestyle section

**Summit**:
The forward-looking destination of the Mountain Journey, representing where Diego is heading rather than a finished or final achievement.
_Avoid_: Finish line, endpoint
